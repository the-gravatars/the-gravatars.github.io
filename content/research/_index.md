## Gravitational-wave astronomy
We search for continuous and long-duration gravitational waves, develop data-analysis methods, and investigate signals that challenge conventional search strategies.

## Neutron stars and compact binaries
Our interests include neutron-star physics, compact-object populations, unusual binary systems, and primordial black holes.

## Dark matter and fundamental physics
We use gravitational-wave detectors and astrophysical observations to search for ultralight dark matter, exotic fields, and new physics.

## An evolving research program
The Gravatars Group is defined by scientific questions rather than a fixed set of source classes. Our research interests will continue to evolve with new discoveries and collaborations.
