    <h2>Andrew L. Miller</h2>
    <p class="profile-position">Associate Professor · ICTP-AP / UCAS</p>
    <p>Andrew L. Miller is an Associate Professor at the International Centre for Theoretical Physics Asia-Pacific (ICTP-AP), based at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) in Beijing, China. His research focuses on gravitational-wave astronomy and using gravitational-wave observations to investigate fundamental physics and the nature of dark matter.</p>
    <p>His interests include gravitational waves from neutron stars, primordial black-hole binaries, ultralight dark matter, and boson clouds around rotating black holes. He develops and applies data-analysis methods to search for weak or unconventional signals in observations from the LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA detectors.</p>
    <p>He earned his PhD in Physics through Sapienza University of Rome and the University of Florida, following an MS in Physics from the University of Florida and a BS in Physics from The College of New Jersey.</p>
